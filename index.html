About Me

I grew up in the Philippines, where I saw firsthand how technology can bridge gaps and transform lives. That inspired my path as a data scientist and developer, building models and tools on geospatial, demographic, and shopper data. I have lived in Manila and Singapore and worked across different fields, from data science and software engineering to product strategy for Southeast Asia and Australia.

I'm now interested in research and open-source work at the intersection of ML, geospatial data, and LLMs. I am privileged to have had a diverse set of experiences, having lived, worked, and played football in five (and hopefully counting) cities.

  • GeospatialPythonMapLibre

    Metro-Mapping

    Open-data pipeline that delineates the functional metro area of any Philippine city on an H3 hex grid. Combines OpenStreetMap establishments and roads, WorldPop population, and a CBD-connectivity rule to produce a reproducible built-up footprint, calibrated against GHSL and validated on held-out cities.   • JavaSwingGeometry

    NGon Word Game

    Word game from a college class where every letter tile is a regular n-gon, n ∈ [4, 16], and n is also the tile's score. Each polygon is constructed from a target area: side length s = √(2·A·tan(π/n)/n), apothem a = s / (2·tan(π/n)), then vertices are walked from the top by turning 2π/n per edge. The apothem doubles as the hit-test radius for clicks, and tile color is a red–blue gradient in n.

    GitHub
